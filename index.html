      How Future Forge works

      Invent a local solution under a rising crisis. Spend scarce resources, clear critics, then field the system. The AI co-inventor advises — you decide.

      The loop

      1. 1 Invent Stack, story, resources
      2. 2 Feasibility Claims fit this year?
      3. 3 Challenge One critic · Defend / Fix / Sidestep
      4. 4 Deploy Pilot → Scale → New normal

      Resources

      On Invent and Challenge. Only AP refills on End turn.

      Year & turn

      Year moves only on Wait. Turn advances on Wait or End turn.

      AP 3 / turn

      Attention for tech, Lobby, challenge entry, AI, Defend / Fix / Sidestep, Deploy. End turn refills AP. Wait burns leftover AP, then refills.

      Budget start 5

      Capital for techs and Pilot / Scale. Same-turn remove refunds half. Not refilled by End turn. Solo: Budget 0$ is game over — keep cash to field.

      Will start 3

      Political will — not a crisis meter. Lobby: 1 AP + 1 Budget → +1 Will. ≥4 boosts deploy; 0 hurts it. Sidestep costs 1 Will.

      End turn vs Wait

      End turn

      Next invent/challenge beat. AP → full. Year and crisis meters unchanged.

      Wait +2 years

      Later world conditions. Crisis meters rise. Leftover AP burned, then refilled. Preview shows the rise. Frontier stacks can risk an extra spike.

      Story faces
      • How it works — mechanism for this place.
      • Everyday life — who feels the change.
      • Focus + Fill other side (1 AP) drafts the other face. Both stay editable.
      AI co-inventor
      • Invent: chips + chat. 1 AP per request.
      • Challenge: chat + Coach / Draft / Ask only (no invent chips). First help free, then 1 AP.
      • You own Defend / Fix / Sidestep on the left.

      Challenge — how you respond

      One random critic. They start with 2 resolve (hearts on their banner — not yours). Empty it to unlock Deploy. Two weak defenses (misses) fail the run.

      Defend in writing

      1 AP to submit

      Write who acts, who pays, or what limit you respect. Strong answer clears them (−2); partial chips 1; miss chips 0 and counts against you.

      Fix the invention

      1 AP · optional +¤1

      You edit how-it-works on Challenge. Apply fix chips 1 resolve (2 if funded = clear). AI coaches; you apply.

      Sidestep once

      1 AP · 1 Will

      Skip without winning. Once per run; softens elegance. Button greys out after use.

      Deploy bay — Pilot · Scale · New normal

      After Challenge, crisis relief is frozen into a pool. Pilot and Scale each roll to succeed — they are not automatic. Odds come from the traffic light: Red 10% · Yellow 50% · Green 85% (green is strong, never certain). Wait stays blocked while the bay is open.

      1 · Pilot

      Each try: AP + Budget · chance = overall feasibility

      First fielding. You pay each attempt. Success: ~half the crisis pool drops; mission counts as solved; Scale unlocks. Fail: no crisis drop — retry Pilot (pay again) or Back to Invent to rework.

      2 · Scale

      Each try: AP + Budget + Will (Will free if Will ≥ 4)

      Wider rollout. Chance uses the Scale feasibility dim only (not the overall light). Success spends the rest of the crisis pool and automatically reaches New normal. Fail: Pilot still stands — retry Scale or go back to Invent.

      3 · New normal

      Automatic when Scale succeeds

      Not a separate button. On Scale success the mission ends: full win if meters are low enough, else partial. Optional +1 crisis drop if Will ≥ 4 and the original pool was ≥ 4.

      ← Back to Invent

      Anytime in the deploy bay

      Rework stack, story, or resources, then return via Back to deploy bay. You do not lose a successful Pilot; failed attempts only cost what you already spent.

      Feasibility dims: Timing, Story, Local fit, Resources, Stack, and Scale. Overall light aggregates all of them (including Scale). Pilot uses the overall light; Scale uses the Scale dim alone. Toasts only say succeeded or failed — not the raw roll.

      Daily · Pins · Share

      • Daily mission on the home screen — same date seed for everyone today. Play or pin it.
      • Pins (max 3) save missions on the home screen for later. Pin from Daily or the mission list.
      • Share card on the outcome screen downloads a PNG of your run (stars, place, stack) — no server required.

      Stars & tips

      • ★★★ for speed, honesty, elegance (outcome only). Sidestep softens elegance.
      • Stay local. End turn often; Wait when later years are worth the crisis cost.
      • Lobby toward Will 4 before Pilot. Save AP for Defend and fielding cost.
      • Feasibility judges claims, not card “unlocks.”
      • Stronger stack, story, challenge quality, and Will ≥ 4 increase the frozen relief pool at unlock — stages only split that pool; they don’t recompute it later.
      • Solo: Budget 0$ is game over — keep capital to field Pilot/Scale.
