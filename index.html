      How Future Forge works

      You invent a local solution for a concrete place under pressure — with emerging tech and an AI co-inventor as your creative partner (not the sole inventor).

      1. Invent — pick a tech stack, name the idea, and tell the story of the invention in two faces (see below).
      2. Feasibility — the red / yellow / green light judges whether your claims fit this year and place. Categories are always choosable; over-claiming is what goes red.
      3. Challenge — defend the idea against a random challenger: Moloch (system game mechanics), Ethicist (hard tradeoffs), Stakeholder (officials & community), or Mother Nature (physical world). The co-inventor can coach or draft an answer if you are stuck.
      4. Deploy — if you pass, crisis meters drop. Too little relief and you can keep inventing.

      How it works ↔ Everyday life

      Your invention has two story faces. Both text boxes stay editable at all times.

      • How it works — the mechanism: what acts, what decides, how the pieces connect for this place.
      • Everyday life — a day after it works: who feels the change, what is easier or safer.
      • The Focus toggle (How it works / Everyday life) selects which face you are emphasizing. The pressed button stays selected; only one is active at a time.
      • Fill other side sits on the focused face. Write that face first, then click it so the AI drafts the other face from your stack and text. You can always edit either box yourself.

      AI co-inventor

      Open the AI co-inventor tab (or the ✦ button) to brainstorm with Grok: spark ideas, suggest a tech stack, name the invention, teach techs, or use Art of the possible for milestones, current capabilities, and use cases. You lead; it proposes. Apply suggestions only when you want them.

      Mission clock (top center)

      Shows the current year and turn. Waiting advances the calendar in multi-year steps and can improve world conditions for stretch ideas — but it is never free.

      Crisis meters (top right)

      Each meter is 0–5: how bad that part of the local crisis is right now. Wait raises them. Deploy after a successful challenge lowers them. Hit 5 on any meter, or the fail year on the clock, and the mission ends.

      Tips

      • Stay local: name who benefits on this street, clinic, or quay.
      • Mix domains when the problem needs it — not as a checkbox.
      • Feasibility judges claims in how it works, not whether a tech card is “unlocked.”
