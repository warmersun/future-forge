How you play

  1. Pick a local scenario — several concrete places living a piece of a global problem.
  2. Invent with AI — pick emerging technologies that fit the local problem (mix domains when it helps — not as a rule).
  3. Watch the years tick — world conditions shift; the crisis gets worse if you wait.
  4. Deploy in time — intercept the future: not too early, not too late — then see that world painted.

      How Future Forge works

      You invent a local solution for a concrete place under pressure — with emerging tech and an AI co-inventor as partner (you lead). Scarce resources and hostile critics make every choice matter.

      1. Invent — spend AP / Budget / Will to build a stack, name the idea, and write two story faces.
      2. Feasibility — red / yellow / green judges whether your claims fit this year. Categories are always pickable; over-claiming goes red.
      3. Scrutiny combat — clear 2–3 challengers (each 2 HP) with Argue, Patch, or Pivot.
      4. Deploy — costs AP + Budget to field the system; crisis meters drop if you succeed.

      Resources (Invent & Challenge top bar)

      • Year & turn — year only moves on Wait. Turn advances on Wait or End turn.
      • AP (3 per invent turn) — attention. Spends on: add tech, Lobby, enter challenge, AI (fill / co-inventor / coach / draft / ask), Argue, Patch, Pivot, Submit/Argue judgment, Deploy. End turn refills AP without raising crisis. Wait burns leftover AP then refills.
      • Budget (start 5) — capital. Tech cards show cost (e.g. ¤1). You must afford a tech to add it. Same-turn remove refunds half Budget. Deploy also costs Budget (ops / fielding — robots in a village still need install money). Challenge success can grant +Budget.
      • Will (start 3, max 5) — political will, not a crisis “Trust” meter. Lobby = 1 AP + 1 Budget → +1 Will. Helps deploy relief (≥4) and can discount fielding cost; Will 0 hurts deploy drop. Pivot spends 1 Will.

      Wait vs End turn

      • End turn — invent round ends; AP refills; year and crisis stay put.
      • Wait (+2 years) — later world conditions; crisis meters rise; leftover AP burned. Preview shows the rise before you click. Frontier-heavy stacks can risk an extra meter spike.

      Story faces

      • How it works — mechanism for this place.
      • Everyday life — who feels the change after it works.
      • Focus picks which face owns Fill other side (AI drafts the other face, 1 AP). Both boxes stay editable.

      AI co-inventor

      Side tab or ✦ button: spark, stack, names, teach, Art of the possible. Each request costs 1 AP. Applying techs still needs Budget/Will.

      Scrutiny combat (Challenge)

      Resources stay visible. Green feasibility → 2 encounters; yellow → 3. Each challenger has 2 HP (Moloch, Ethicist, Stakeholder, Mother Nature). The portrait that appears first for an encounter stays fixed.

      • Argue (1 AP) — write a defense; AI scores hit (−2 HP), glance (−1), or miss (0). Two misses fail scrutiny.
      • Patch (1 AP) — revise how-it-works under fire (−1 HP), or spend +1 Budget for a funded patch (−2 HP).
      • Pivot (1 AP · 1 Will) — clear that encounter once per run (−15 elegance later).
      • Coach / Draft / Ask — first help in a scrutiny run is free; then 1 AP each.
      • Clear all encounters to unlock Deploy.

      Deploy cost & crisis drop

      • Fielding cost: 1 AP + Budget (base ¤1; +¤1 if 3+ techs; +¤1 frontier-heavy stack; Will ≥ 4 can shave ¤1 when cost would be higher). Button shows the cost.
      • Crisis drop: stronger stack, story, challenge quality, and Will ≥ 4 increase relief; Will 0 reduces it. Hit 5 on any crisis meter or the fail year → mission ends.

      Outcome stars

      Up to ★★★ for speed (few Waits / early year), honesty (feasible claims), and elegance (synergies). Pivot softens elegance. Stars appear on the outcome screen only.

      Tips

      • Stay local: who benefits on this street, clinic, or quay.
      • End turn often; Wait only when later years are worth the crisis cost.
      • Lobby toward Will 4 before deploy for mandate bonuses.
      • Save AP for Argue/Deploy — don’t burn it all on freeform AI chat.
      • Feasibility judges claims, not whether a card is “unlocked.”
