How you play

  1. Pick a Quest — several concrete places living a piece of a global problem.

  3. Watch the years tick — world conditions shift; the crisis gets worse if you wait.
  4. Deploy in time — intercept the future: not too early, not too late — then see that world painted.

                How Future Forge works

                Pick a place in trouble. Build an honest invention from emerging tech. Answer one hard question. Field it before the crisis wins. The AI co-inventor advises — you lead.

                Three steps

                1. 1 The place is in trouble Watch crisis meters and the year. Wait raises the crisis; stall and the place can fall.
                2. 2 Invent something honest Pick emerging tools for this place. Write what it does here — claims must fit this year.
                3. 3 Answer a hard question, then field it Face one challenge, then Field it before meters max or the fail year.

                Loop: Invent · Challenge · Field. Feasibility is honesty about timing — not a card unlock gate.

                More detail (Workshop systems)

                Resources

                Full Workshop HUD. Only AP refills on End turn. First-run Spark hides most of this.

                Year & turn

                Year moves only on Wait. Turn advances on Wait or End turn.

                AP 3 / turn

                Attention for tech, Lobby, hard question, scrutiny moves, Field, and the AI co-inventor (1 AP per AI request). End turn refills AP. Wait burns leftover AP, then refills.

                Budget start 5

                Capital for techs and Pilot / Scale. Same-turn remove refunds half. Not refilled by End turn. Solo: Budget 0$ is game over — keep cash to field.

                Support start 3

                Public + policy support — not a crisis meter. Lobby: 1 AP + 1 Budget → +1 Support. ≥4 boosts fielding; 0 hurts it. Sidestep costs Support equal to remaining resolve hearts.

                End turn vs Wait

                End turn

                Next invent/challenge beat. AP → full. Year and crisis meters unchanged.

                Wait +2 years

                Later world conditions (tech and readiness can mature). Crisis meters rise. Leftover AP burned, then refilled. Preview shows the rise.

                Story
                • Spark: one box — What does it do here?
                • Workshop: How it works + Everyday life.
                • Workshop: Focus + Fill other side (1 AP) drafts the other face.
                AI co-inventor 1 AP each

                Not free — every AI request spends 1 Action Point.

                • Invent: chips, chat, Fill other side — 1 AP each.
                • Challenge: Coach / Draft / Ask — 1 AP each.
                • You still decide stack, story, and whether to field.

                Hard question — Workshop scrutiny

                Spark is essay-only. Workshop may open Defend / Fix / Sidestep against one critic’s resolve hearts.

                Defend in writing

                1 AP to submit

                Write who acts, who pays, or what limit you respect. Strong answer clears them (−2); partial chips 1; miss chips 0 and counts against you.

                Fix the invention

                1 AP · optional +¤1

                You edit how-it-works under fire. Apply fix chips resolve; AI coaches, you apply.

                Sidestep once

                Cost = remaining ♥

                Skip without winning — once per run. Cost tracks remaining resolve (AP + Support). Softens elegance stars.

                Field — Pilot · Scale · New normal

                Spark: one Field it button. Workshop: Pilot then Scale after the hard question. Odds: Red 10% · Yellow 50% · Green 85%. Wait blocked while the bay is open.

                1 · Pilot

                Each try: AP + Budget · chance = overall feasibility

                First fielding. Success drops ~half the crisis pool; Scale unlocks. Fail: retry or rework on Invent.

                2 · Scale

                Each try: AP + Budget + Support (Support free if ≥ 4)

                Wider rollout. Success spends the rest of the pool and reaches New normal. Fail: Pilot still stands.

                3 · New normal

                Automatic when Scale succeeds

                Mission ends: full win if meters are low enough, else partial.

                ← Back to Invent

                Stars & tips

                • ★★★ for speed, honesty, elegance (outcome only). Sidestep softens elegance.
                • Stay local. End turn to refill AP; Wait when later years are worth the crisis cost.
                • Lobby toward Support 4 before Pilot when economy is on.
                • Feasibility judges claims, not card unlocks.
                • Solo: Budget 0$ is game over when Budget is active.
