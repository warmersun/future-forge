Future Forge

Invent local solutions with emerging tech — beat the clock as the future arrives.

How you play

  1. Pick a local scenario — several concrete places living a piece of a global problem.
  2. Invent with AI — pick emerging technologies that fit the local problem (mix domains when it helps — not as a rule).
  3. Watch the years tick — world conditions shift; the crisis gets worse if you wait.
  4. Deploy in time — intercept the future: not too early, not too late — then see that world painted.

      How Future Forge works

      Invent a local solution under a rising crisis. Spend scarce resources, survive hostile critics, then field the system. The AI co-inventor advises — you decide.

      The loop

      1. 1. InventStack, story, resources
      2. 2. FeasibilityClaims fit this year?
      3. 3. ChallengeClear 2–3 critics
      4. 4. DeployPay to field; crisis drops

      Resources

      Shown on Invent and Challenge. Only AP refills on End turn.

      Year & turn
      Year moves only on Wait. Turn advances on Wait or End turn.
      AP (3 / turn)
      Attention. Spend on tech, Lobby, enter challenge, AI, Defend / Fix / Sidestep, Deploy. End turn refills AP (crisis stays). Wait burns leftover AP, then refills.
      Budget (start 5)
      Capital for techs (card cost ¤) and for deploy fielding. Same-turn remove refunds half. Not refilled by End turn.
      Will (start 3)
      Political will — not a crisis “Trust” meter. Lobby: 1 AP + 1 Budget → +1 Will. ≥4 boosts deploy; 0 hurts it. Sidestep costs 1 Will.

      End turn vs Wait

      End turn
      Next invent/challenge beat. AP → full. Year and crisis meters unchanged.
      Wait (+2 years)
      Later world conditions. Crisis meters rise. Leftover AP burned, then refilled. Preview shows the rise. Frontier stacks can risk an extra spike.

      Story faces

      • How it works — mechanism for this place.
      • Everyday life — who feels the change.
      • Focus + Fill other side (1 AP) drafts the other face. Both stay editable.

      AI co-inventor

      • Invent: chips (spark, stack, Art of the possible, …) + chat. 1 AP per request.
      • Challenge: chat + Coach / Draft / Ask only (no invent chips). First help free, then 1 AP.
      • You still own Defend / Fix / Sidestep on the left.

      Challenge — how you respond

      Green feasibility → 2 challengers; yellow → 3. Each has 2 HP. Pick a response mode (it stays selected), then complete the step under it.

      Defend in writing

      1 AP to submit

      Write who acts, who pays, or what limit you respect. Hit −2 HP · glance −1 · miss 0. Two misses fail the run.

      Fix the invention

      1 AP · optional +¤1

      You edit how-it-works on Challenge (not back to Invent). Apply fix deals 1 HP (2 if funded). AI can coach; it does not apply the fix for you.

      Sidestep once

      1 AP · 1 Will

      Skip this challenger without winning. Once per run; softens elegance score.

      Clear all challengers to unlock Deploy.

      Deploy

      • Fielding cost: 1 AP + Budget (base ¤1; +¤1 if 3+ techs; +¤1 frontier stack; Will ≥ 4 can shave ¤1). Button shows the cost.
      • Crisis drop: stronger stack, story, challenge quality, Will ≥ 4 increase relief. Meter at 5 or fail year → mission ends.

      Stars & tips

      • ★★★ for speed, honesty, elegance (outcome screen only). Sidestep softens elegance.
      • Stay local. End turn often; Wait when later years are worth the crisis cost.
      • Lobby toward Will 4 before deploy. Save AP for Defend and Deploy.
      • Feasibility judges claims, not card “unlocks.”
