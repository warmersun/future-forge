# Commercial licensing — Future Forge

Future Forge is **source available**, not OSI open source.

**Free:** personal, non-commercial self-host only (see `LICENSE.md`).  
**Paid license required:** schools, companies, nonprofits, multi-user deployments, and any cloud / managed service.

> Draft commercial terms for product clarity. Final pricing and contracts should be reviewed by a lawyer.

---

## Who needs a paid license?

| Situation | Free personal? | Needs paid license? |
|-----------|----------------|---------------------|
| You alone, hobby learning, own machine | Yes | No |
| Use at work / for a client / in a product | No | **Commercial** |
| School, university, bootcamp, training org | No | **Education** |
| Multi-user classroom or org server | No | **Education** or **Commercial** |
| You host Future Forge for other people / tenants | No | **Cloud / Managed Service** |
| White-label or embed as SaaS | No | **Cloud / Managed Service** (and/or Commercial) |

If you are unsure, assume you need a license and contact us.

## License tiers

### 1. Education

**For:** schools, universities, accredited programs, non-profit training (not internal corporate L&D unless agreed).

**Typically includes:**
- Right to run self-hosted Future Forge for enrolled learners
- Optional: seats on an official hosted classroom (if offered)
- Curriculum / facilitator notes (if provided under the agreement)

**Does not include:** reselling hosting to other institutions without a Cloud tier.

### 2. Commercial

**For:** companies, freelancers using it in paid work, product teams embedding or operating Future Forge for employees or customers (single-tenant / internal).

**Typically includes:**
- Right to self-host for the licensed entity
- Internal training and product R&D use as defined in the order form
- Optional support / update window

**Does not include:** multi-tenant public SaaS competing as “Future Forge cloud” without the Cloud tier.

### 3. Cloud / Managed Service

**For:** anyone offering Future Forge (or a derivative) as a hosted, multi-tenant, or managed service to third parties.

**Typically includes:**
- Right to operate a commercial cloud offering
- Branding rules (use of “Future Forge” name may require a separate trademark license)
- Revenue share or annual fee as specified in the order form

## What you get with any paid license

- Written permission covering your use case (entity, term, territory)
- Clarity for procurement / compliance
- Optional: priority support, custom scenarios, SSO, or private deployment help (quoted separately)

You still supply your own **xAI / SuperGrok** credentials for self-host, unless you buy a **hosted** offering where we run the API path for you.

## Hosted Future Forge (our cloud)

Separately from self-host licenses, we may operate an official hosted product:

- Learners and orgs subscribe without running Node themselves  
- AI usage metered or included per plan  
- Terms of service apply in addition to this commercial framework  

Self-host under Education/Commercial is still available for orgs that prefer on-prem.

1. Email or open a GitHub issue with subject **Commercial license** (see contact below).

3. We send an order form / agreement and invoice.  
4. On signature (and payment, if required), you receive a license confirmation.
