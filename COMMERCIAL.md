## Additional free grants (no paid license required)

In addition to rights under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, Warmer Sun Education grants the following **free, worldwide, non-exclusive** permissions. These grants do **not** require a paid commercial license. They do **not** expand the CC grant for other commercial uses (selling access, charging fees, embedding in a paid product, white-label SaaS, etc.).

### 1. Host a meeting (you are present as a participant)

You may run a Future Forge session as a **live meeting** — in person or online — for friends, family, classmates, community groups, clubs, or similar gatherings, provided:

- **You are present and participate** in the meeting (not an unattended kiosk, unattended classroom lab, or fully automated drop-in service); and  
- You do **not charge** participants for access to Future Forge or for the meeting itself as a Future Forge product (optional tip jars / unrelated venue fees do not by themselves make this commercial for this grant); and  
- You keep any required **attribution** reasonable (e.g. product name / link to the project).

Examples that **are** covered: a friends co-op night on a shared call; a facilitator-led workshop where the host also plays; an online hangout you run and join.

Examples that **are not** covered by this free grant alone: hiring out facilitation as a paid service, selling tickets primarily for Future Forge play, or any school / university / bootcamp / training-org use (Education is commercial — see below).

### 2. Run a free online server (no commercial intent)

You may **operate a Future Forge server on the public internet** (or a shared network) for yourself and others to use, provided:

- You have **no commercial intent** in operating the service; and  
- You do **not charge** anyone for access, seats, API usage through your instance, or “Future Forge hosting”; and  
- You do **not** market the instance as a paid or freemium product, multi-tenant commercial cloud, or white-label SaaS.

Examples that **are** covered: a free public demo instance you maintain as a hobby; a free community server with no fees; Tailscale Funnel / reverse-proxy access for friends with no charge.

Examples that **are not** covered: charging subscriptions, selling seats, monetized multi-tenant hosting, or operating the service as part of paid work for a client.

These free grants may be revoked only for a specific operator who violates them (e.g. starts charging); they do not terminate CC rights for compliant non-commercial use of the materials.

## License tiers

### 1. Education

**Education is commercial use.** Schools, universities, bootcamps, and training organizations need a paid **Education** license. Free CC use and the free grants above do **not** cover institutional teaching or training.

**For:** schools, universities, accredited programs, bootcamps, non-profit training (not internal corporate L&D unless agreed).

**Typically includes:**
- Right to run self-hosted Future Forge for enrolled learners
- Optional: seats on an official hosted classroom (if offered)
- Curriculum / facilitator notes (if provided under the agreement)

**Does not include:** reselling hosting to other institutions without a Cloud tier.

### 2. Commercial

**For:** companies, freelancers using it in paid work, product teams embedding or operating Future Forge for employees or customers (single-tenant / internal).

**Typically includes:**
- Right to self-host for the licensed entity
- Internal training and product R&D use as defined in the order form
- Optional support / update window

**Does not include:** multi-tenant public SaaS competing as “Future Forge cloud” without the Cloud tier.

### 3. Cloud / Managed Service

**For:** anyone offering Future Forge (or a derivative) as a hosted, multi-tenant, or managed service to third parties.

**Typically includes:**
- Right to operate a commercial cloud offering
- Branding rules (use of “Future Forge” name may require a separate trademark license)
- Revenue share or annual fee as specified in the order form
