# Sensitivity

For themes involving violence, persecution, slavery, FGM, genocide, etc.:

- Use concrete **institutional** places (clinic, school board, jetty desk) — not gore spectacle.
- Stakeholders with dignity; no real victims’ identities.
- Inventable local systems (early warning, records integrity, safe logistics) — not revenge fantasies.
- When in doubt, pick a less charged theme that still teaches the emTech advance.
