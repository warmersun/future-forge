# Brief template (`mission.briefMd`)

Prose: `references/scene-prose.md`. Short. Cut anything the reader does not need.

The invent screen shows **one card per paragraph**, **Your job** last, merged down to **8** cards. A blank line starts a new card. The co-inventor reads the first **2800** characters. The 12 000-character cap is a ceiling, not a target. Lint warns above **600** words.

```markdown
## The place

One instance of the global problem. As short as clarity allows. One paragraph is enough.

## The bigger problem

What the global problem is, in everyday words. Why this instance keeps happening, if that is not already clear.

## Your job

What must get better. No product. No "invent with [tech]." No "do not invent X." No "pass a law" / "ban" / "UBI" as the win.
```

Headings in that order. Do not add **What just became possible** or **Constraints**. Old heading **What's strained** still walks; do not use it on new tiles.

`mission.scene` is the instance again, in plain text, ≤500 characters, able to stand alone. `summary` is the instance on the card. `title` is the situation or the place. `encourageCopy` is the outcome.
