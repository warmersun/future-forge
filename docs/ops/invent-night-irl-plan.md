Status: draft  
Send/post: no until you approve  
Host: Sic / Warmer Sun  
Product on every table: Future Forge (`https://warmersun.com/forge/`)

This is the weekly in-person night. It is **practice**, not a way to fill a funnel. A listing does not invent friends. Count people who come back, not RSVPs.

---

## Promise

**For people who already like do-meetups** (they come if a friend is going), who believe making and showing is how you belong:

You learn what a few emerging technologies are actually good for. Then you invent **with other people** on a hard story in Future Forge. You cannot get that brainstorm alone at home. You do not have to finish the Quest. You can have a couple of minutes in front of the room.

You do **not** work on your own app. You do **not** sit through a lecture. You do **not** need homework.

### Value (what they walk out with)

| They wanted | What this night gives |
|---|---|
| A gift they would otherwise pay for | The AI co-inventor is **on us** for that hour (you pay the bill so it does not die mid-round) |
| To *do*, not listen | Two Quests, 20 minutes each |
| To be seen | Small room: anyone who wants ~2 minutes after the break. Later: 5-minute Ignite slots when people *ask* |
| Food and beer | Pizza or donuts, beer if the room allows — in the **break**, not a dinner |
| Friends / a scene | Break + linger. The night does not create the first friend; you still have to bring one |
| Zero prep | Bring a laptop. Sit where the story grabs you |

**This / not that**

- Inventing on a story, not vibe-coders “my repo”
- A conversation on the board, not a TED
- Stuck is a legal finish, not a failed demo
- Future Forge every time; **holding** the Quest is optional
- Insights on the mic count

**Culture line:** People like us open a hard story and invent together.

**One-line invite (when you have someone to text):**

> Free pizza. We open a hard story in Future Forge. The AI is on us for an hour. You don’t have to finish. You get two minutes if you want them.

## Who it is for (and who it is not)

**For:** People who already go to do-meetups, will open a laptop, and will try a story if the first sentence is interesting.

**Not for:** People who only want to code their own project. People who want a speaker series. Schools as the customer. Anyone who needs a deck to walk in.

Vibe-coders is a **place to meet people**, not the copy on the door.

## The night (same clock at 3 people and at 30)

About 90 minutes. Weekly. You host.

### Beginning (few people, nobody prepared) — this is the default

No lightning talks. You are at the table, not on a stage.

| Block | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Arrive | 10 min | Pizza/donuts. Laptops open. No program. |
| Learn (not a lecture) | ~5 min | Open the Quest. Someone reads the three lines: **job, place, what’s strained.** Each person picks **one technology** on the tray and says what they think it is good for *here* (30–60 seconds). You add one sentence if they stall. Then you shut up. |
| Round 1 | 20 min | Same Quest. Invent on the board and/or argue why it is hard. Both count. |
| Move | 2 min | Some people change tables (or you open the second story). |
| Round 2 | 20 min | New Quest, **same theme**, different place. Same rules. |
| Break | 10 min | Food and beer. People talk to each other, not to you. |
| Show | ~15 min | Anyone who wants ~2 minutes: an invention, an insight, or “why this is hard.” Timer visible. |
| Linger | leftover | Next week’s date on a board. No closing speech. |

**Two rounds. Not three.** A bigger crowd means more tables, not more program.

**You say this once**, after the three-line brief, so nobody thinks they failed:

> We are using Future Forge. You do not have to finish the Quest. If you get stuck or you only have an argument, that is still the night. You can say that at the end.

### Later unlock (only when 2–3 people out of a real 20–30 *ask* for a slot)

Ignite-style: 5 minutes, visible timer, they sit even if they are not done. Talk must feed **tonight’s stories** (what this tech is good for). Up to three slots at the **front**. End show stays short. Do not leave empty speaker chairs “for the format.”

### Tables

- 3–4 people per table.
- 3 people is already a full night (one table, you sit with them).
- 6–12: two or three tables.
- 20–30: more tables. **Show time stays ~15 minutes.** Each table sends one person; leftover time is extra hands.

### What is on the table

Every laptop opens Future Forge. Completing the Quest is optional. Notes apps, sketches, and side projects are **not** the night.

The host picks **two stories people would lean into after one sentence** — not the theme title. Play both yourself first. If *you* are bored, they will be.

Theme one (on the door as inventing; on the table as a scene): **Totalitarianism** as the shelf, never as the lecture title.

**Example first Quest** (already in the game): Omar’s night school. The tutor dies mid-sentence when someone asks a rights question. Three learners quit. They still need the certificate. Invent a way the room can help without sending words out of the building.

**Second table:** a different place, same shelf (nurse’s “Unreliable” family, vendor locked out of the cold locker, power cut on one side of the street). Sit where the sentence grabs you.

If Wi‑Fi dies: paper copy of the three-line brief only. Not a craft kit.

Host prep: two Quest links ready, AI budget for 40 minutes so the co-inventor does not go dumb, pizza.

## How the room grows (do not skip stages)

Growth is **more tables + more returners**, not a bigger poster.

| Stage | Size | What you do | What you do *not* do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — You + 1 or 2** | 2–3 | Run the full night. You are at the table. Ask one person you already see at vibe-coders (after *you* take two minutes there): “Wednesday, invent on a story, pizza.” | No Luma. No three speakers. No 50-seat room. |
| **1 — A table that returns** | 4–8 | Same clock. Second table only if you have a second unsticker or the first table is stable. Seed names from rooms you already attend. | Do not “improve the poster” if nobody came back. |
| **2 — A small listing** | 8–12 | List only after you can name ~5 people who said yes (Luma’s Vancouver surface wants sign-ups). Job in the title, Future Forge in the body. Approval off. | Meetup.com (you already rejected it). Title that is only “Future Forge.” |
| **3 — Reputation** | 20–30 | More tables. Same two rounds. Ignite only if people asked. 5–10 regulars is the real win. Occasional people on top. | 50+ (different night). Sponsor coupons. A third Quest. |

**Metric:** people who came **and** said they will come next week.  
Four weeks with no returner: shrink. Do not add talks.

**Still on you:** the first friend. Credits, pizza, and a good story do not replace a text.

## Venues

Need: quiet enough to think, tables, power, Wi‑Fi, one screen (or laptops are enough at 3 people), food nearby or allowed, beer if possible. Free or “you look good / you bring people” — not a dinner bill.

**Do not use:** restaurants and pubs (too noisy). Time Out Market as the work room (same problem). VPL Central if you need food (no food). Meetup.com as the channel.

### Ask first (klout / in-kind)

**1. Progression Cafe** — 275 E 10th Ave (Mount Pleasant).  
Vibe-coders already meet Saturday mornings here. Licensed cafe, food and beer, public cafe (no gym pass required for the cafe). Weeknight **tables in a quiet enough corner**, or a booked group if they have a screen. They already want community programming.  
Ask: weeknight 90 minutes, 3–4 tables, laptops, not a climbing class.  
Site: https://www.progressionbouldering.com — cafe notes also at https://www.progressioncafe.com

**2. Northeastern University Vancouver** — 1400 – 410 West Georgia St.  
They say volunteer-run tech meetups (AI, software, etc.) may apply for **in-kind classroom/event space** if there is some connection to their community. Classrooms, AV, downtown. Food/beer: ask; campus rooms often restrict alcohol.  
Start: https://vancouverevents.sites.northeastern.edu/  
Campus: https://vancouver.northeastern.edu/contact/

**3. FDU Vancouver at Oakridge Park** — 210-5968 Cornelia Mews (Canada Line: Oakridge–41st).  
New campus, classrooms, computer labs, they want industry and community in the building. Food and beer **after**, downstairs at the mall — not inside Time Out as the invent room. Opening stretch includes industry nights (mid-October 2026). Pitch a student-open invent table, not a product pitch.  
Campus: https://www.fdu.edu/campuses/vancouver-campus/welcome-fdu-vancouver/fdu-vancouver-oakridge/

**4. UBC** — possible, slower, often not free for a company night.  
Learning Commons rooms want UBC-open learning events, not a closed company meetup. Alumni Centre and GSS rooms are paid public rentals. Only chase if a UBC person will sponsor the booking.  
https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/about/workshop-space/

### Backup (honest rent, small)

- Coal Harbour Community Centre meeting room: tables, projector, on the order of **$55/hour** (confirm current rate). Quiet, not a pub.  
- A 15-person meeting room near Oakridge on Peerspace-class listings (~$40/hour) if klout asks stall and you still want SkyTrain.

### Venue by stage

| Stage | Room |
|---|---|
| 0–1 (2–8 people) | Progression cafe corner **or** one FDU/Northeastern classroom. Do not rent a hall. |
| 2 (8–12) | Classroom with tables + one screen. Food in break in the room or next door. |
| 3 (20–30) | Same kind of room, more tables. Northeastern event space or FDU multipurpose **only** when the regulars exist. |
