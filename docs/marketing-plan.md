## Change

**We seek to help adults who can feel the take-off but have no framework for it become rational techno-optimists: people with a mental model of how emerging technologies compound, converge, and reach real places, and the agency to invent with them rather than watch.**

What's it for? To replace "everything is changing and I cannot keep up" with a working map: eleven families, the curves each one rides, what converges with what, and the discipline of inventing a pathway that intercepts a curve at the right time for a real place. The change is measured in people who hold a pathway, come back for the next Quest, and can explain the map to someone else.

What it is **not** for: teaching a tool, issuing a certificate, replacing school STEM, calming people down, or letting an AI invent on the learner's behalf.

## Smallest viable market

**Audience name: the take-off aware.** Adults who have noticed, mostly through AI, that the rate of change itself is changing. They are not asking whether it is real. They are asking what to do with the feeling.

Who's it for (psychographics, not averages):

- They already believe the acceleration is real. They have watched a capability go from impossible to routine inside a year and felt the ground move.
- They oscillate between wonder and vertigo. Some days it is thrilling; some days it is a wall of headlines with no handles.
- They are neither doomers nor dismissers. They reject "it is all hype" and they reject "it is all over." They want a third position and cannot articulate it yet.
- They want agency. Being informed is not enough; they want to be someone who can act on what is coming.
- They are comfortable in a browser with a laptop. They will not install Node. The hosted table at warmersun.com/forge is their door.
- They read about advances the day they land. That is when they are most open.

Shared problem / language / urgency:

- Problem: "I can see it accelerating. I have no framework for it, so I cannot tell which advances matter, how they connect, or what I could do."
- Language they already use: take-off, acceleration, exponential, curve, "this is happening faster than I expected," convergence, singularity (some), "what does this unlock." They do not say emTech yet. They will after one Quest.
- Urgency: internal and real. The feeling that things are moving and they are standing still. The product does not need to manufacture it; it needs to give it somewhere to go.

Where they gather / what they already trust:

- Wherever a specific advance is being discussed the day it lands: comment threads, forums, group chats, the newsletters and podcasts they already follow about AI and frontier tech. This is a hypothesis, not a finding. See First touch tests under Assertions.
- They trust a demonstration over a description, and a framework over a take. Opening a link and seeing the curve beats reading another opinion.
- People who share the optimism without the naivety: Kurzweil, Diamandis, and the intellectual current the product doc already names. The 6Ps and the 6Ds nod are in the product's bones.

Nonbelievers we will shun:

- **Schools and teachers.** `docs/warmer-sun-cloud.md` already says no classroom UX. A school is a licence conversation under `COMMERCIAL.md`, not a marketing target. Copy stops addressing them.
- Dismissers who believe the acceleration is a bubble and want to be told so. The product will not tell them that, and they will not invent.
- Doomers who want the framework to conclude that nothing can be done. Rational techno-optimism is the stance; the challengers (Moloch, Mother Nature, Ethicist, Stakeholder) are in the room to make the optimism honest, not to cancel it.
- People who want AI to produce the invention for them. The invent gate is "you lead."

Not shunned, not the market: parents (the live page names them) and companies (potential sponsors and licensees). They can find their way in. The page and the promise are not written for them.

The live page currently addresses parents, college students, and professionals at once and calls the product a learning platform. Its headline, "Change isn't constant. It's accelerating," already speaks to this market. The rest of the page should follow the headline.

## Worldview, status, tension

Worldview they already believe:

- "We are in the take-off. AI proves it, and it is not only AI."
- "Being informed is not the same as understanding. I read everything and still cannot connect it."
- "There must be a way to think about this that is neither panic nor dismissal."
- "I would rather be someone who does something with this than someone it happens to."

We do not argue with any of this. We hand them the framework their worldview is missing: eleven families, bits and atoms and bridges, the curve each family rides, convergence as the engine, timing as the discipline, and the pathway as the unit of agency.

Status: **affiliation first, light dominance second.** Holding a pathway says "I am one of the people who understands what is happening and is doing something with it." That is an identity the audience already wants and cannot yet claim. Per-quest leaderboards add a playful dominance layer: honesty stars divided by years, one row per person, write-up on every row. Ranking stays playful. Do not send dominance signals (win, beat, crush) to affiliation people.

Tension (rubber band) and how action releases it:

- Before first touch: "You can feel it. Can you explain it? Can you act on it?" Opening a Quest and seeing Look Ahead plot the curve releases the first half. Holding a pathway releases the second.
- Inside the session: the place can fall. Wait advances the curve and heats the crisis. Too early is vapor; too late the place has fallen. Declaring the hold releases it.
- In Friends rooms: shared place, shared fate. If nobody Scales, everybody loses. Real tension between people, not fear.

Pattern match or pattern interrupt? **Pattern match on the news habit.** They already click through on a breakthrough. The Quest is one more click on the same impulse. The interrupt is that this click hands them a framework and asks them to invent, instead of another take.

Fear we will not exploit: job loss to AI, existential risk, climate collapse, children's futures. The audience already carries these. Quests instantiate real harms honestly, but the marketing never uses dread as the hook. The promise is a framework and agency, and the stance is optimism that has passed the challengers.

## Promise

**For adults who can feel the take-off and have no framework for it, Future Forge builds the mental model to make sense of rapid change, eleven families, their curves, their convergences, and the timing discipline to invent with them, by having you invent one honest pathway for a real place in one sitting, so you leave as a rational techno-optimist with agency rather than a spectator with vertigo.**

Falsifiable test: a first-timer who opens a Quest can, at the outcome screen, name the families they used, say which curve mattered and why the timing was honest, and state the pathway in one sentence. If most cannot, the promise is false and the first session changes before any further reach.

Feature vs benefit vs proof vs feeling:

| Layer | What it is |
|-------|-----------|
| Feature | Eleven emTech families in a tray, Look Ahead curves with doubling, halving, and CAGR chips, reached and predicted milestones, convergence when tiles touch, honesty bar for timing, four challengers, hex pathway board, AI co-inventor, Friends rooms with shared crisis, Spotlight Quests from real advances, Foresight for your stack at the outcome |
| Benefit | A map you can hold in your head, a way to tell which curves are steep now, and a pathway you invented and can defend |
| Proof (what exists today) | The core table and open invent quests run free (no account needed to invent); learning/lesson quests need free signup; Look Ahead plots real capability curves per family; the co-inventor grades timing against the current year; four Quest tiles exist, two pegged to specific advances. **No completion, retention, or satisfaction numbers exist.** |
| Feeling | Orientation ("I finally have a frame for this"), agency ("I could actually do something with it"), earned optimism at the hold, belonging when a friend is in the room |

This / not that ("better for someone"):

- A framework you practise, not a newsletter you read.
- Rational techno-optimism, not hype and not doom. The challengers are what make the optimism rational.
- Eleven families and their curves, not "just AI."
- A pathway that rides the curve, not a solution frozen on today's tools.
- Timing literacy: intercept the curve, not too early, not too late.
- You lead, AI advises. Not an AI that invents for you.
- An inventing practice, not a learning game and not a course.
- For individuals and friends, not for classrooms.

## Remarkable edge

What is worth remarking on *to this market*:

1. **Look Ahead.** The exponential clock, per family, plotted for the stack you actually picked, with the line for now and the line for after you Wait. For someone who feels the acceleration and has never seen it drawn for the technologies in their own idea, this is the moment the framework becomes visible. It is the first clip in the friend cut for a reason.
2. **Convergence when tiles touch.** Batteries make better drones, more drones demand better batteries. The audience has heard the word convergence. Watching two hexes catalyze each other, with honesty bars ticking up, is the first time most of them will have seen it work.
3. **Timing as a discipline.** The honesty bar and the Timing check do not say "your idea is hype." They say "this is a 2030 capability and you are inventing in 2026; wait for the curve or write the claim for now." That is the rational half of rational techno-optimism, made into a mechanic.
4. **A Quest about the advance in the news, the same week.** A Spotlight tile can be authored from a paper or announcement by any AI agent using `skills/future-forge-quest/`, validated, and shared as one link. The framework meets the headline while the headline is still warm.
5. **Shared fate with personal inventions.** Two to six friends, one crisis, own inventions. If the place falls, nobody wins.

What a first-timer would tell a friend, in one sentence, unpaid: *"It put the gene-sequencing news on an actual curve, made me invent what a clinic would do with it, and told me which parts are 2026 and which are 2030."*

If nothing lands as remarkable after the first three Quest drops, change these before spending on reach:

- The first five minutes must show Look Ahead for the family the Quest is about, before anything else. The framework is the hook; do not bury it behind a tour.
- The Quest brief must name the advance and the curve in its first line so the link matches the news the person just read.
- Guarantee the hosted AI quota for the drop window. "Shared host AI while tokens last" is honest but is the single biggest way a first try can feel broken.

## Tribe and culture

People + leader + idea:

- **People:** the take-off aware who have held at least one pathway. Connected to each other through Friends rooms and through the write-ups on each Quest's leaderboard.
- **Leader:** Sic / Warmer Sun, by name. Someone has to author the first Quests, post the first links, and read every write-up. The agent is not the leader.
- **Idea:** *Invent on the curve. Not too early, not too late.* Already the series sting in the recording pack. It is the framework in six words.

Culture line: **People like us put the breakthrough on the curve and invent with it, the week it lands.**

Secondary culture rules the product already enforces and marketing echoes: optimism that has faced the challengers; eleven families, not one; a pathway, not a solution; you lead, AI helps; if the place falls we all lose.

How members talk to each other (not only to us):

- The per-quest leaderboard shows every row's pathway write-up. Members read each other's inventions on the same job. This is the primary member-to-member surface and it is already shipped.
- Friends rooms are member-to-member by design: layering emTech onto another seat's stack, trading, allying.
- Members can host Friends rooms under the free meeting grant in `COMMERCIAL.md`. A tribe with several hosts is a tribe; one with a single hub is a list.
- Discord `#inventions` (one sentence per invent) remains available as a rented room for those who want it.

Spread design (sneezers / smoothness / hive), not "go viral":

- **Hive:** the conversation around a specific advance in the week it lands, among people who already feel the acceleration. The Quest enters that conversation as "here is the curve, here is what you would do with it," not as a product announcement.
- **Promiscuous sneezers:** people who held a pathway and had the Look Ahead moment. Their tool is the outcome share card, Foresight for your stack, and the `?q=` link. Their status gain: they are the friend who has a framework when everyone else has a feed.
- **Powerful sneezers:** people who author Quest tiles from real advances (researchers, newsletter writers, agent tinkerers) and people who already publicly hold the rational techno-optimist position. Their tool is `skills/future-forge-quest/`, the validator, and the economy simulator. Their status gain: a Quest card and a board with their authorship on it. Do not pay them or dress them as ads.
- **Smoothness:** one link opens one specific Quest. Open invent quests need no account; learning/lesson quests and sponsored spotlights need free signup (see [Access wording source of truth](docs/warmer-sun-cloud.md#access-wording-source-of-truth)). One sentence transmits the idea. The remaining friction is the hour it takes, so drops land when people have time.
- **What we will not do that would make sneezers turn against us:** fake numbers, mandatory sign-in on the hex engine, a teacher dashboard, letting the AI invent for people, letting a sponsor's product name count as a solution, or sliding into either hype or doom in the copy.

### Assertions (we do X → Y happens; tension; model)

1. If a Spotlight Quest about a real advance is published within days of that advance and shared as one link where people who feel the acceleration are discussing it, then some open it, the Look Ahead moment lands, and a measurable share finish a hold. This is the core bet. Untested.
2. If the outcome screen offers Cloud save at the moment of pride and, once built, a one-line opt-in to the new-Quest notice, then a meaningful share of people who hold a pathway say yes, and that list is the owned asset.
3. If the second Quest drop goes to that list first, then return from the list beats return from any rented platform, and the plan stops depending on where the news is discussed.
4. If Quests are visibly authored from real advances with honest grounding and a rational-optimist stance, then a company whose product category is in the news sees a Quest as a better sponsorship than an ad: players invent applications in their category, the sponsor line is text only, and the product name is never the answer. Cold outreach after three public drops has a chance; before any drops it has none.
5. If first-timers see Look Ahead for the Quest's family within five minutes and hit one timing verdict, they repeat the "it put it on a curve" story unprompted. That story is what spreads.
6. If Friends rooms are offered as the second visit, return improves over solo replay.

**First touch tests (owner undecided).** Run each for three drops, log opens, holds, and Cloud saves per source, then keep the one that produced holds:

| Test | Where the link goes | What we learn |
|------|--------------------|---------------|
| A. News-pegged Quest drop | The thread or forum where the advance is being discussed, once, as a reply with the one sentence and the link | Whether people who feel the acceleration will invent with it |
| B. Personal Friends-room invites | Two to five people the owner knows who talk about the take-off, ad hoc, into a shared-crisis room on the same Quest | Whether the friend path converts and whether Friends is the return hook |
| C. On-the-curve clip plus link | One clip from the friend cut per drop, Look Ahead first, posted with the Quest link | Whether seeing the curve drawn beats reading about it |

Business model, in order: free Quest drops and free table build the list; Cloud accounts and quest boards make it a place people return to; sponsored Spotlight Quests fund AI tokens (a sponsor pays for authoring and hosting, players stay free); a Cloud plan and paid modules later, once Billing is on; commercial licences to companies and paid hosts when they ask. Education licences are paperwork when a school asks, never a target.

Tension in the model: the free hosted AI is both the hook and the cost. The plan accepts spending tokens on public drops before any revenue, with a per-drop quota, for one quarter, as the price of testing assertions 1 to 3.

### Alternatives (if assertions fail)

- If three drops produce opens but almost no holds: the first session fails, not the framework. Put Look Ahead first, cut the brief, start on a docked tile. Do not add content.

## Ethics

True / consent / opt-out / claims needing evidence:

- **True:** every number on the site or in a post comes from the usage logs, the quest boards, or the drop log. No invented attendance, no "learners in N countries," no fake sponsor logos. "Free," "you lead, AI helps" are always true; "no account needed to invent" is true for open invent quests and the core table (see [Access wording source of truth](docs/warmer-sun-cloud.md#access-wording-source-of-truth)).
- **Stance honesty:** rational techno-optimism means the challengers are real. Copy never promises that technology fixes the problem; it promises a framework and a practice for inventing pathways that might. Predicted milestones are forecasts, never played as unlocks. The recording pack's "do not say" list applies to all copy.
- **Consent:** the new-Quest notice is opt-in, one line, at the outcome screen, with the promise stated. Nobody is added because they signed in. Links posted into other people's threads go once, as a reply that adds something, never as a spray. Ask moderators where rules require it. No scraped lists, no cold DMs to strangers.
- **Opt-out:** one click off the notice; account deletion cascades rows via the Clerk webhook; leaving Discord is one step. Nobody is chased.
- **Young audience:** the mission names high-school seniors. The market here is adults. Marketing does not target minors or collect their contacts. Cloud already has display names, hidden email, and reporting.
- **Sponsorship:** the schema's rules are the ethics. Text only. Grounding holds category-level truth. The product name is never a valid solution. Sponsorship never changes scoring, feasibility, or AI prompts. A sponsor who wants otherwise is declined. Sponsored Quests are labeled everywhere they appear.
- **Claims needing evidence before use:** any statement about learning outcomes, "mental model," or "agency" as achieved results; completion or retention rates; the honesty bar's accuracy. Until measured, describe the mechanism and the practice, not the outcome.
- **Sensitive content:** Quests instantiate real harms. Marketing never uses those harms as fear hooks.
- **AI honesty:** the co-inventor can be wrong. Say so. Never claim the AI knows the future.
- **Status respected:** ranking stays playful and per-quest. No public shaming. Never rank lesson counts or token use.
