## Change

We seek to help people who are fascinated by tech and how it’s advancing become people who have actually learned how that progress works — by inventing on a real problem, not by finishing a lesson.

What's it for? **Learning.** Specifically: the exponential progress of emerging technologies. It is built to make that learning as playful and fun as possible, because nobody has to take this subject. Fun is the method. Learning is still the job. Inventing on a real problem is how the learning happens. It is not what the work is for.

They leave able to say what they tried, who it was for, and whether it is ready yet — because that is how you can tell the learning stuck. Reciting a taxonomy is not the learning. Finishing a game is not the learning.

What it is not for: teaching a single tool, a certificate, school STEM as a department, calming people down, a philosophy identity, or an AI that does the work for them.

## Smallest viable market

**Who:** people who are fascinated by tech and how it’s advancing. Age does not decide who is in. Someone young who is fascinated is in. Someone older who is not, is out.

Who's it for (psychographics, not averages):

- They would say something like: “I’m fascinated by tech and how it’s advancing.”
- They already believe it is real. They are not waiting to be convinced.
- They bounce between “this is amazing” and “I am only watching.”
- They do not want to be told it is all hype, and they do not want to be told it is all over.
- They already know reading is not the same as doing something with it. They still spend the hour on more articles.
- They will use a browser. They will not install Node. warmersun.com/forge is the door.
- They read the news the day it lands. That is when they are most open.

Shared problem / language / urgency:

- Problem they would say: “I keep reading about this stuff and I never do anything with it.”
- Also: “Okay but what do you actually use this for?” and “Is this even ready yet?”
- Words they already use: “this is happening faster than I expected,” “what does this unlock,” acceleration, exponential, curve. Some say take-off. Nobody says mental framework, honest invent, pathway, hold, emTech, or hex.
- Urgency: the feed already supplies it. The hour needs somewhere to go besides another article.

Where they gather / what they already trust:

- Wherever a specific advance is being discussed the day it lands. This is still a hypothesis. The 2026-09-19 HN reply was never sent, so we have no hold-from-thread number.
- They trust a demonstration over a description. Opening a link and seeing the curve beats another take.

Nonbelievers we will shun:

- Schools and teachers as a marketing target. A school is a licence conversation under `COMMERCIAL.md`, not a campaign.
- Dismissers who want to be told it is a bubble.
- Doomers who want the framework to conclude that nothing can be done.
- People who want AI to produce the invention for them.

Not shunned, not the market: parents-as-a-segment (the live page still names them) and companies (possible later payers). The page and the promise are not written for those roles. Learner and payer can differ: a parent may pay; the person who invents is the one who already feels the take-off.

The live page still addresses Parents / Campus / Work in one breath, still tags “invent local pathways,” and still calls the product a learning practice. Its headline (“Change isn’t constant. It’s accelerating”) already speaks to this market. The rest of the page has not followed. Page rewrite is not GTM and is not the next ship.

## Worldview, status, tension

Worldview they already believe (things they would say):

- “This is happening faster than I expected.”
- “Reading another article isn’t going to change that.”
- “I don’t need a lecture that it’s real.”
- “If I’m going to spend an hour I want to have tried something, not finished a lesson.”

We do not argue with any of this. We do not sell them a worldview. We give the hour something to try.

Status: **affiliation, by opening a room that was closed.** Today, studying exponential progress — and inventing with it — is treated as a high-ticket practice. Peter Diamandis’s Abundance360 (what the owner calls the platinum club) sells that room to founders and investors at $12,500–$55,000 a year, application only. The status quo is: people like *those* people do this. The shift is: people who are fascinated by tech and how it’s advancing get to do the same kind of work — learn by inventing a pathway — without buying the club. They do not become billionaires. They do not join A360. They get the practice that club was selling as scarce.

What they tell a friend is still the idea they came up with, not “I joined a cheap Abundance.” Example: “What if the clinic ran the sequence on the shift, before the lab truck even leaves?” They would not say “I held a pathway.” They would not recap Future Forge. Per-quest boards are playful ranking only. Do not send win/beat/crush. Do not sell “now you can be like Peter’s members.” Do not put Diamandis on the public door until the owner says to (still an open decision below).

Tension (rubber band) and how action releases it:

- Before first touch: “You already think this is speeding up. Have you actually used any of it?” The band releases when they have tried it on a real problem, not when they can name categories.
- Inside the session: the place can run out of time. Waiting makes it worse. Finishing and saying it holds is the release.
- With friends: same place, same clock.

Pattern match or pattern interrupt? **Pattern match on the news habit.** They already click through. The interrupt is that this click is trying it, not another article.

Fear we will not exploit: job loss, the end of the world, children’s futures, “you are falling behind.” The hook is a sentence they already say: they keep reading and they never do anything with it.

## Promise

For people who are fascinated by tech and how it’s advancing, who keep reading and never do anything with it, Future Forge is play that teaches how emerging technologies actually progress: you pick a real problem, invent with this week’s new thing, the computer does not do it for you, and you find out which parts are ready now. You came to learn. Inventing is how. Both belong on the door.

Falsifiable test: they can tell a friend what they tried, who it was for, and whether it is ready yet. Listing categories is not the test. **This test has not been run in public.** Zero logged tries from marketing drops.

Feature vs benefit vs proof vs feeling:

| Layer | What it is |
|-------|-----------|
| Feature | Open table, a job in a place, hex board, Look Ahead, honesty bar, four hard questions, AI that advises, Friends rooms |
| Benefit | You actually used it on something real, and you know which parts are now |
| Proof (today) | Core table runs free with no account. Remote tiles with unset `access` require free signup ([access wording](docs/warmer-sun-cloud.md#access-wording-source-of-truth)). Look Ahead plots real curves. Four Quest tiles exist in-repo; none carries a sponsor. **No completion, retention, or satisfaction numbers exist. The 2026-09-19 drop was not posted.** |
| Feeling | “I figured something out.” These problems are hard. Yellow lights can hold; making everything green is rare. Pride is earned. Not “I am an inventor.” Not “I have a framework.” |

This / not that:

- Learn by inventing, not another article, not a course, not a game you win.
- Use it first. The map shows up while you use it. Not: study, then you may try.
- You do it. The computer advises. Not: the computer does it for you.
- Built so it gets better as the tools get better, not a one-shot on today’s tools.
- Hard questions in the room, not hype, not doom.
- More than AI when the problem needs it. Not a taxonomy lesson.
- Using new tools on a real problem, not “local” as the category.
- For people who will try it, not for classrooms.

## Remarkable edge

What is worth mentioning to a friend is **the revelation**, not the app.

They learn the exponential trend. They see a prediction about what is coming in three to five years, or sooner. They see a way humanity actually gets somewhere huge — cure cancer, not a feature list. That is what they remark about. Future Forge is the avenue that got them there. They mention the app only after the revelation, if they mention it at all.

What a friend hears, unpaid, sounds like: “We can cure cancer. I saw a way we get there.” Or: “I didn’t realize this was that close.” They do not hear “I tried Future Forge.” They do not hear “I learned a framework.” They do not lead with the pathway as a product recap. The pathway is how they saw the way. The remark is the way.

The sitting has to produce that seeing:

- Look Ahead and the predictions are how the trend becomes visible — what doubles, what halves, what is ready in a few years.
- Inventing on a real problem is how they connect that trend to a human destination (a clinic, a ward, a crop). Learning by inventing.
- The quests are hard. Yellow can hold; all-green is rare. If they figured something out, they have a right to be proud — that pride is for them. The friend still hears the revelation, not the score.

What we will not water down:

- Real trends and dated predictions, not vibes.
- Hard problems. Do not make all lights green for free.
- You invent. The computer advises. If the model does it, there is no seeing and nothing to tell.
- Job / place / what’s strained on the brief. No capability lecture.
- Learn by inventing on the door. Not a course first. Not a game you win.

Sic inventing in public is not what friends repeat. It is how a stranger might see a revelation this week. Friends rooms are later, once a second person exists.

If after three public sittings nobody is talking about what is coming — only about the board — change the sitting before spending on reach. Ads are not how this becomes worth mentioning.

Future Forge does not claim to cure cancer. If a learner sees a way and says that to a friend, that sentence is theirs. Copy never promises the destination is guaranteed.

## Permission asset

Permission is they expect to hear from us again and would notice if we stopped. It is not a follower count. It is not an email list. We do not have email. We do not use Discord.

What they would miss if we vanished:

- **The next sitting that shows what is coming** — Sic, twice a week, on X, with a link to the open table. That is rented land (X). Useful only if the sitting actually produces a revelation. Weak until they come back to the table on their own.
- **Warmer Sun Cloud** after they have seen something: save, Continue, a page that is theirs. Sign-in is offered after pride, never at the door of the open table.
- **warmersun.com** and a `?q=` link to one Quest. Owned.

Promise in exchange for coming back (one sentence, and then only this until they grant more):

*When a curve moves, you get one sitting that shows what is coming — a trend, a few-year prediction, a way through — and a link to try it. Nothing else.*

No product news. No upgrade pitch. No survey. If a sponsor appears later, their name is on the Quest card, not in this drip.

Ladder — smallest next yes:

| Rung | Who | Smallest next yes |
|------|-----|-------------------|
| Stranger | Saw a sitting or a link | Open the **open table** (no account). See a trend or a prediction. Invent is how they see it. |
| Friend | Saw a way; has a pathway they are proud of | Save to Cloud at the outcome screen |
| Customer | Needs to run it for work, school, or hosting | Personal self-host stays free. Commercial — including education — is a paid license. Hosted login is Cloud when it exists. Some content may be paywalled. Sponsored Quest or white-glove if that is the job. |
| Loyal | Comes back when the next curve moves | Another sitting. Maybe they write a Quest. |

X is not permission. It can introduce. Discord is not in the stack. Email is not in the stack.

How we know it is real: they come back for the next sitting, or they save after they saw something. Followers and likes are not recorded. If we stopped posting and nobody asked where we went, we never had permission.

## Tribe and culture

A tribe is people, a leader, and an idea. It is not ours to command. If the only hub is Warmer Sun, it is a list.

The **broader tribe** is already there: **e/acc** and **edu/acc** — people who believe accelerating technology is real, mostly good, and something you can learn and build with, not only watch or fear. Future Forge does not found that tribe. It is a table inside it.

The **tighter group** we hope grows:

- **Around group sessions** — Friends rooms, shared clock, same place. Not a public night campaign. Invent Night is not GTM. If people show up to sit together, that is the group.
- **Around online sharing** — they tell the revelation (what is coming, a way humanity gets there). They mention the app after, if at all. Site shares and X. Not Discord. Not email.

- **People:** those who are fascinated by tech and how it’s advancing; who already speak, or would speak, in that e/acc / edu/acc current; who have seen a trend, a few-year prediction, a way through.
- **Leader:** Sic. The agent is not the leader. Someone has to go first, in public, twice a week.
- **Idea:** this subject is learnable. You learn it by inventing on a real problem. You do not have to buy a high-ticket room to see what’s coming.

People like us: **we don’t only pass the article. We look at the curve, invent a way, sit with others when we can, and tell what we saw coming.**

How they talk to each other (not only to us):

- Group sessions: Friends rooms (already shipped). Same place, same clock.
- Online: write-ups on the per-quest board (already shipped); shares of the revelation, not a recap of the hexes.
- Discord is not a surface in this plan. X is where a stranger might first see Sic sit.

How it spreads (not “go viral”):

- **Where:** conversations that already exist in that broader tribe, especially the week an advance lands.
- **Who repeats it:** people who saw what is coming. They tell that. They mention the app after, if at all.
- **Who writes Quests later:** not week 1. Do not pay people to pretend they care.
- **How easy:** one link opens the open table, or one Quest that needs no account. Do not send strangers to a signup-gated lesson first. The remaining friction is the hour.
- **What would make them stop:** fake numbers, a forced sign-in on the open table, a teacher dashboard, the computer inventing for them, a sponsor’s product as the answer, hype or doom in the copy, pretending we own e/acc.

## Ethics

- **True:** numbers come from logs or boards. No invented attendance, no fake partners, no “learners in N countries.” Access copy follows `docs/warmer-sun-cloud.md`. “No account needed to invent” is true for the open table and for tiles with `access: "open"` only.
- **Revelation vs claim:** copy never promises Future Forge cures cancer or that technology will fix the problem. If a learner sees a way and tells a friend, that sentence is theirs. Predictions are forecasts, not unlocks.
- **Consent:** one reply in a thread that already discusses the advance. No spray, no scraped lists, no cold DMs to strangers. No pitch in the public post.
- **Opt-out:** leaving X or deleting a Cloud account is enough. Nobody is chased. No email list to leave.
- **Age:** not who it is for. Do not run a campaign at children. If a young person sits, they are in. Ongoing 1:1 with a minor only if a parent asks after the kid wanted the try, and only after a vulnerable-sector check.
- **Sponsorship:** text only, labeled. The product name is never the answer. Never touches scoring or the advisor.
- **Do not claim until measured:** that people “learned,” completion rates, that the honesty bar is accurate.
- **The advisor can be wrong.** Never claim it knows the future.
- **Status:** ranking stays playful and per-quest. Do not sell “now you can be like Diamandis’s members.”
